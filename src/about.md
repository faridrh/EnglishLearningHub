## What is LeaTalk?

LeaTalk is a language-learning product focused on steady progress: a little practice each day beats long sessions you never finish. The [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.leatalk.app) puts lessons in your pocket, while web sites in the project give you full courses and practice:

- **[İngilisdili.com](https://ingilisdili.com)** — free English courses (A0–A2), vocabulary, grammar, stories, quizzes, and games
- **[EasyDanish](https://www.easydanish.com)** — Danish for English speakers, beginner through advanced, with stories, quizzes, and mini-games
- **[LeaTalk website](https://leatalk-website.vercel.app/)** — product hub for structured vocabulary paths and language tools

## Why learners choose us

- **Structured learning** — lessons and words in a clear sequence so you build confidence instead of collecting random flashcards
- **Practice that sticks** — courses, quizzes, stories, and games so you meet language in more than one way
- **Learn anywhere** — use the [LeaTalk Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.leatalk.app) on the go, then continue on the web when you want a longer session
- **Made for real beginners and beyond** — levels from zero to pre-intermediate (and more on other LeaTalk sites) so you always know what to study next

## How to get started

1. Explore free English courses on **[İngilisdili.com](/courses/)** — start at A0, A1, or A2.
2. [Download LeaTalk from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.leatalk.app) and complete your first short practice session.
3. Open [www.easydanish.com](https://www.easydanish.com) if you want to learn Danish week by week.
4. Follow [@leatalkofficial](https://www.instagram.com/leatalkofficial/) for motivation, tips, and news about new content.
5. Visit the [LeaTalk website](https://leatalk-website.vercel.app/) for more product resources.

## Ready when you are

Whether you have five minutes on your phone or a quiet evening at the desk, LeaTalk, İngilisdili.com, and EasyDanish are here to help you move forward — one word, one lesson at a time.
